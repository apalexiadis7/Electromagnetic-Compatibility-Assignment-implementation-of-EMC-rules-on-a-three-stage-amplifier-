This work is about the design of a three-stage amplifier implementing all the basic EMC rules. The final PDF contains three chapters: a more general theoretical part, a part with results from HFSS simulations and the final part with a proposed design of the amplifier.
This work was assigned to us for the course of Electromagnetic Compatibility in Aristotle University of Thessaloniki by professors Nikolaos Kantartzis and Dimitrios Karatzidis.  
Grade: 10/10
